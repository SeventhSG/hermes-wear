# Hermes Companion privacy

_Updated September 2026_

Hermes Companion has no Stormlabs account, analytics, advertising or telemetry backend. It connects to the self-hosted services you configure. Your Hermes server may in turn use its own models and tools; check that server's configuration.

## What leaves the phone

- **Hermes API:** Your prompts, dictated transcripts, selected/shared text, chat attachments (including photos), replies, session and job changes, and API key go to your configured Hermes server. When enabled, each turn also carries time zone, device model, language, battery and network state; approximate location and screen text/screenshot are separate opt-ins. Phone queries send requested calendar entries, matching contact names/numbers, app names, next alarm, battery, approximate location or clipboard text back to Hermes. Calendar, contacts and clipboard queries require their own opt-ins and Android permissions where applicable. Phone context is sent in an ephemeral turn message, though your server controls its own logging.
- **hermes-hub:** If configured, the app sends authenticated event/search/host-ops requests, push endpoint registration, document uploads and test notifications to your hub. Hub event notifications (including jobs, briefings and proposals) return through the hub. UnifiedPush hands the push endpoint and event payload through your selected distributor, such as ntfy and its configured server; without push, Android JobScheduler polls your hub for events.
- **Capture inbox:** Notes, shared items, photos, files and voice captures you submit go to the separately configured Essentials capture server with its token. Failed voice captures can remain in app-private storage for retry.
- **Speech recognition:** Voice input records audio only while you invoke it, unless you enable the wake word. On-device mode keeps audio on the phone and uses an optional multilingual Whisper tiny model stored in app-private files. The model downloads from GitHub only when you choose Download in Settings, and you can delete it there. Server mode sends PCM to your self-hosted Wyoming Whisper endpoint (or the Hermes host on the default Whisper port). Auto uses the on-device model when installed, otherwise the server. Dictation text goes to Hermes only when you submit it. Spoken replies are passed to your chosen installed Android TTS engine; the app prefers a non-Google engine when available. Your engine's own privacy behavior depends on that engine.
- **Wake word:** While **Hey Hermes** is on and listening, the microphone stays open; Android shows its microphone indicator and a persistent notification. Audio used only to listen for the wake word is processed on the phone by the sherpa-onnx keyword model and never sent anywhere. Its roughly 18 MB model downloads once from GitHub. After "Hermes," the command recording (which can include the end of the wake word) is transcribed by your selected speech recognizer (on-device or your own Whisper server). Its transcript goes to your Hermes server like any voice message.
- **Reply drafting:** With both notification-listener access and Reply drafting enabled, the app reads notifications from only your allowed apps on the phone. Notification text goes to Hermes only after you tap **Draft reply**. A draft is sent through Android's notification reply action only after you tap **Send**; **Edit** opens it for review.

## Controls and storage

Device actions and basic phone context are enabled by default and can be switched off. Location, screen context, calendar, contacts, clipboard, reply drafting, app allow-list entries, push and Places require separate opt-ins or Android permissions. Places proximity checks and saved place coordinates stay on the phone; arriving or leaving may trigger a Home command sent to Hermes. Sensitive phone actions require device authentication or a visible confirmation. Calls, SMS and email open a prefilled app; you still place or send them. The Permission centre has a master kill switch and a local activity log you can inspect or export.

Each profile's API key is encrypted at rest using Android Keystore. One-tap profile import fetches keys from your own hub over the existing hub connection. The app also stores preferences, drafts, place settings, activity history and session cache in its private storage; conversations and scheduled jobs reside on your server. Use HTTPS or a trusted private network for your configured endpoints. No app data is sent to Stormlabs unless you explicitly configure a Stormlabs-operated endpoint.

Hermes's `RecognitionService` can serve other apps only when Android routes their speech recognition requests to it and they hold `RECORD_AUDIO` permission. It uses the selected on-device or self-hosted Whisper recognizer.
